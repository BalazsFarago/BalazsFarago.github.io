Balázs Faragó

I am a PhD candidate in economics at Central European University. My research interests include the economics of information and how economic agents like us humans interact with it. Currently, my main area of focus is information acquisition. Specifically, I am working on topics such as the interplay of top-down and bottom-up attention allocation, as well as questions regarding the value of information across multiple decision problems. These topics have various applications (e.g. microfoundations to macroeconomic models, political economics, industrial organisation, finance) but applications regarding media markets and other "markets for information" are of especially high importance since media consumption is itself a form of informaiton acquisition with spillover effects to other markets, social learning, and culture.
